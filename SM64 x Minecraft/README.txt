SUPER MARIO 64 x MINECRAFT
==========================
The real Super Mario 64 (built from the SM64 decompilation and your own US ROM)
with Minecraft building, blocks, UI, sounds and lighting (from your
minecraft-assets-26.2 folder) added on top. Windows 10/11, DirectX 11.

Double-click "SM64 x Minecraft.exe". The mouse is captured when you play;
press Esc to pause and free the cursor, click the window to capture it again.

CONTROLS (keyboard + mouse)
  Move ............ W A S D
  Jump ............ Space
  Crouch / pound .. Left Shift      (SM64 Z button)
  Punch / dive .... F, or left-click when not aiming at a block
  Look ............ Mouse            (arrow keys also rotate / zoom)
  Break block ..... Hold left mouse
  Place block ..... Right mouse (hold to keep placing)
  Pick block ...... Middle mouse
  Hotbar .......... 1-9 or mouse wheel
  Block inventory . E               (click a block to put it in your hotbar,
                                     or hover and press 1-9)
  Camera mode ..... F5   third person -> first person -> classic SM64 Lakitu
  Debug info ...... F3
  Hide HUD ........ F1
  Pause ........... Esc or Enter
  Fullscreen ...... F11 (or Alt+Enter)

CONTROLLER (Xbox)
  Left stick move, right stick look, A jump, X/B punch, LT crouch,
  RT break, Y place, LB/RB hotbar, D-pad up = inventory,
  D-pad down = camera mode, D-pad left = pick block, Start = pause.

HOW THE TWO GAMES MIX
  * Blocks are solid for everything: Mario, Goombas, Bob-ombs, coins and the
    camera. Wall-kick off them, ledge-grab their edges, build bridges and
    staircases to reach stars.
  * Mario's punches and kicks chip blocks away (soft blocks break in one hit).
  * Ground-pounding smashes glass, ice and leaves.
  * TNT: punch it or ground-pound it to light it. It blows up nearby blocks and
    hurts Mario like a Bob-omb blast. Bob-omb explosions break blocks too.
  * Slime blocks bounce Mario back up and cancel fall damage.
  * Ice and packed ice are slippery, soul sand is sinky, magma burns.
  * Glowstone, sea lanterns, shroomlights and jack o'lanterns light up the area.
  * HUD: hearts = Mario's power meter, air bubbles while swimming,
    XP bar = coins toward the 100-coin star, green number = stars.

SAVING
  Your builds are saved per SM64 save file and per level/area in the
  "mcsm64_saves" folder next to the exe (autosave every 30 s and on exit).
  SM64 progress is saved as usual. Settings are in "mcsm64config.txt"
  (mouse_sensitivity, invert_mouse_y, start_in_first_person, key bindings).

This build contains Nintendo and Mojang assets from your own copies of the
games, so keep it for personal use and don't share it publicly.
